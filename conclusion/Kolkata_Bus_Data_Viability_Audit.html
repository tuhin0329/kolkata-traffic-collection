Longitudinal Investigation across 40 Kolkata Corridors (Sept 03–22, 2026) | Diagnostic Analysis of Day-of-Week Speed Elasticity

Scientific Finding & Verdict: Google Maps Bus Data is a Static Timetable Schedule, NOT Real-Time Probe Telemetry

Across 40 arterial corridors observed over 20 consecutive days, the characteristic speed of public buses demonstrates a near-total absence of day-to-day variance. 23 out of 40 corridors exhibit EXACTLY 0.00 km/h variance across Monday through Sunday. On the remaining 17 corridors, variance is limited to minor timetable adjustments (±0.3 km/h). Unlike passenger cars and motorcycles—which dynamically fluctuate based on crowdsourced GPS probes—Google Maps transit estimates in Kolkata are derived from pre-programmed General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) static timetables without real-time Automatic Vehicle Location (AVL) sensor integration.
